Idle Car Empire – Terms of Use

Apply to the Idle Car Empire apps for Android and iOS. Last updated: 19 October 2026.

In short: play fair and be respectful. The names you choose are seen by other players, so keep them free of insults, hate, sexual content and impersonation. Report players who don't: we may rename or remove them. In-game money and items have no real-world value.

1. Agreement

Idle Car Empire ("the game") is made by an independent developer publishing as zazochannel-art. By creating an account you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, don't create an account.

2. Your account

  • Playing needs an account: a player name, an email address and a password. Keep your password to yourself; you are responsible for what happens with your account.
  • The game is not meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age for online services in your country).
  • You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time: in the game, Profile → Delete account, or on the account deletion page.

3. Names and what other players see

Other players see your player name, avatar, company name, your alliance's name, tag and emblem, and your public empire card. These must not contain:

  • insults, harassment, threats, or hate against anyone for who they are (origin, religion, gender, sexuality, disability…);
  • sexual, obscene or vulgar words;
  • praise of violence, terrorism or extremism;
  • someone else's identity, or a name posing as the game's staff (such as "admin" or "moderator");
  • personal information (phone numbers, addresses), links, advertising or spam.

The game and its server refuse some words automatically; the filter can't catch everything, so the rules above apply either way.

4. Fair play

Don't cheat: no edited saves, no exploiting bugs on purpose, no automated play, no extra accounts to collect gifts or alliance rewards, and no attempts to break or overload the game's servers. Leaderboard totals are checked against what the game allows.

5. Reporting and hiding players

  • Report a player: open them from the leaderboard (or the flag next to an alliance member) → Report, and choose why. Report an alliance's name with the flag next to it in the alliance list.
  • Hide a player: Hide player on the same card. They no longer appear on your leaderboards; show them again from your Profile → Hidden players.
  • Reports go to the developer, who reviews them regularly. For anything urgent, write to idlecarempire@gmail.com.

6. What the developer may do

When a name or a player breaks these terms, the developer may rename the player, hide their company name, close their alliance, take them off the leaderboards, or, for serious or repeated cases, delete the account. Whenever possible the smallest step that fixes the problem is taken first.

7. In-game money and items

The game's money, stars, rewards and items are part of the game only: they have no real-world value and can't be sold, traded or exchanged for money. The game sells nothing for real money. Rewarded ads are always optional. Game balance (prices, rewards, limits) may change in updates.

8. The service

The game is offered as it is. Your progress is kept on your device and in your account's cloud save, but the online features (accounts, cloud save, leaderboards, alliances) may sometimes be unavailable, and features may change or end. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under the consumer law of your country.

9. Changes

If these terms change, the new version is posted here with a new date.

10. Contact

idlecarempire@gmail.com
