Idle Car Empire – Privacy Policy

Applies to the Idle Car Empire apps for Android and iOS. Last updated: 17 October 2026.

In short: the game keeps your progress on your device and, so you can keep it across devices, in its cloud save. The app offers optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob (you choose to watch one for a bonus). The game has no analytics SDKs and doesn't sell your data. You can delete your account and everything stored with it from the game (Profile → Delete account).

Who is responsible

Idle Car Empire is made by an independent developer, publishing as zazochannel-art. Contact: idlecarempire@gmail.com.

The game itself does not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, phone number, advertising ID or payment details. It has no chat and no in-app purchases. The ad provider (Google) uses some device data to show ads: see Ads in the app.

Notifications. If you turn on notifications in Settings, the app asks your phone to remind you of things in your game (a building finished, the offline limit reached) while it is closed. They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Ads in the app

The app can show rewarded ads: short videos you choose to watch for a bonus (twice the offline earnings, a construction finished, or double profit for 30 minutes of play). No ad plays unless you tap to watch one, and nothing in the game requires it.

The ads come from Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, Google collects from your device: the advertising ID (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking), your IP address (from which it estimates an approximate location), device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version), how you interact with the ads, and crash and performance diagnostics. Google processes this data under its own policies: see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

  • Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks first (Google's consent form) and shows personalised ads only if you agree; otherwise the ads are limited or non-personalised. Change your choice any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Your advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads). On iPhone the app asks first (Apple's “Allow tracking?” question); if you don't allow it, the ads don't use your advertising ID. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • The game's own data (your save, account and profile) is never given to Google or any advertiser. The ads are not personalised from your game progress.

Playing offline

If you play without a connection, nothing leaves your device until the connection is back. If you never connect, your game stays on your device only.

Where the data is kept

The cloud save, accounts and leaderboards run on Supabase (database and authentication), which processes the data on our behalf. Like any online service, its servers see your IP address when the game connects; we don't store it in the game's data. On your device the game keeps its data in the app's own storage. This policy and the account deletion page are hosted on GitHub Pages (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each player's save can be read and written only by that player; other players only ever see the public empire card described above.

How long it is kept

Your cloud data is kept until you delete your account. The anonymous progress steps contain nothing that identifies you and are kept as statistics. Data on your device stays until you delete the game's data or uninstall the app (the phone's own backup, to your Google account or iCloud, may keep a copy of the app's data, under your control).

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Profile → Delete account → Delete for good. This deletes your account, cloud save, profile, public empire card, gifts and alliance seat at once.
  • Without the game: see how to request deletion.
  • On your device: uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone's settings.

Your rights

You can see your data in the game, correct your name, leave the leaderboards, switch off the anonymous statistics, and delete everything (above). If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK you also have the rights of the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) and may complain to your data protection authority; write to the contact address above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.

Politica de confidențialitate (Română)

Pe scurt: jocul îți păstrează progresul pe dispozitiv și, ca să-l poți continua pe alte dispozitive, în salvarea din cloud. Aplicația oferă reclame opționale cu recompensă de la Google AdMob (alegi tu să vezi una pentru un bonus). Jocul nu are SDK-uri de analiză și nu vinde datele tale. Îți poți șterge contul și tot ce e stocat cu el din joc (Profil → Șterge contul).

  • Progresul jocului: pe dispozitiv și, dacă joci cu cont sau ca oaspete conectat, în cloud.
  • Un identificator de cont (aleator) pentru cont sau oaspete; emailul și parola doar dacă îți faci cont (parola e stocată criptat de furnizorul de autentificare).
  • Numele de jucător și avatarul, și o fișă publică a imperiului (nume, companie, fabrici, totaluri, cea mai bună mașină de curse) pentru clasamente, vizite, cadouri și curse cu fantome, cât timp ești listat (se poate opri din Profil).
  • Alianța, cadourile și tipul dispozitivului (ex. „Android” sau „iPhone”) cu fiecare salvare.
  • Notificări (dacă le pornești din Setări): telefonul îți amintește de lucruri din joc cât e închis (o clădire terminată, limita offline atinsă). Sunt programate pe telefon; nu se trimite nimic nimănui.
  • Pași anonimi de progres (numele unei etape de început și minutele de joc), trimiși fără cont sau identificator; se pot opri din Setări.
  • Reclame: reclame cu recompensă de la Google AdMob, doar când alegi să vezi una. Google colectează de pe dispozitiv ID-ul de publicitate (pe iPhone doar dacă permiți urmărirea), adresa IP (locație aproximativă), informații despre dispozitiv și aplicație, interacțiunile cu reclamele și diagnostice, pentru afișarea și măsurarea reclamelor și prevenirea fraudei (cum folosește Google aceste date). În SEE, Marea Britanie și Elveția aplicația cere mai întâi acordul; îl poți schimba din Setări → Opțiuni de confidențialitate pentru reclame. Datele jocului tău nu sunt date niciodată Google sau altor agenți de publicitate.

Datele din cloud sunt găzduite de Supabase și păstrate până la ștergerea contului. Ștergere: în joc, Profil → Șterge contul, sau cerere de ștergere. Contact: idlecarempire@gmail.com.
