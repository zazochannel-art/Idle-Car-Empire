3D models used in Idle Car Empire
=================================

gt-r34.glb — the MC GT platform (Performance car)
  Original: "Nissan R34 Brians Fast Furious | www.vecarz.com" by vecarz
  https://sketchfab.com/heynic
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/nissan-r34-brians-fast-furious-wwwvecarzcom-412aa249c2f548eebf64bdb35f3553af
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

  Changes: geometry simplified and meshopt-compressed, textures resized to
  512 px WebP (gltf-transform optimize). In the game the badges and the
  manufacturer plate are hidden; the body is repainted only when the player
  picks a colour. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nissan or the makers of
  the Fast & Furious films; all trademarks belong to their owners.

car-pack.glb — City Car, Sedan, SUV, Luxury, Supercar, Sports Car, Hypercar and
  Electric platforms, and the street traffic (compact, minivan, off-roader, pickup, wagon)
  Original: "Generic passenger car pack" by Comrade1280
  https://sketchfab.com/comrade1280
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/generic-passenger-car-pack-20f9af9b8a404d5cb022ac6fe87f21f5
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

  Changes: meshopt-compressed, textures resized to 512 px WebP (gltf-transform
  optimize); two textures of the Wagon body, missing from the received file,
  replaced with plain grey. In the game each car is taken out of the pack,
  straightened and scaled, and its paint texture is turned grey so the body
  can be painted in the game's colours. The car in the game's logo (the app
  icons, the launch screens, assets/logo.webp) is a render of the Hypercar.

Every model here allows commercial use (CC BY 4.0). Models under a
non-commercial licence (CC BY-NC) are not used: the Android app shows ads.
