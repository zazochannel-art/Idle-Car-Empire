3D models used in Idle Car Empire
=================================

car-pack.glb — every car in the game: the City Car, Sedan, SUV, Luxury, Supercar,
  Sports Car, MC GT, Hypercar and Electric platforms, and the street traffic
  (compact, minivan, off-roader, pickup, wagon)
  Original: "Generic passenger car pack" by Comrade1280
  https://sketchfab.com/comrade1280
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/generic-passenger-car-pack-20f9af9b8a404d5cb022ac6fe87f21f5
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

  Changes: meshopt-compressed, textures resized to 512 px WebP (gltf-transform
  optimize); two textures of the Wagon body, missing from the received file,
  replaced with plain grey. In the game each car is taken out of the pack,
  straightened and scaled, and its paint texture is turned grey so the body
  can be painted in the game's colours. The car in the game's logo (the app
  icons, the launch screens, assets/logo.webp) is a render of the Hypercar.

The cars are generic, unbranded designs (no real make or model). Every model
here allows commercial use (CC BY 4.0). Models under a
non-commercial licence (CC BY-NC) are not used: the Android app shows ads.
