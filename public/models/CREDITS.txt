3D models used in Idle Car Empire
=================================

sports-1975.glb — the Sports Car platform
  Original: "Porsche 911(930) Turbo 1975 | www.vecarz.com" by vecarz
  https://sketchfab.com/heynic
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/porsche-911930-turbo-1975-wwwvecarzcom-1b576d8b56e445fe8595e1b3fc07bcfe
  Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

  Changes: geometry simplified and meshopt-compressed, textures resized to
  512 px WebP (gltf-transform optimize). In the game the badges, stickers,
  number plates and air freshener are hidden and the body paint is
  recoloured. This adapted model is shared under the same licence
  (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Non-commercial use only. Not affiliated with or
  endorsed by Porsche AG; all trademarks belong to their owners.

gt-r34.glb — the MC GT platform (Performance car)
  Original: "Nissan R34 Brians Fast Furious | www.vecarz.com" by vecarz
  https://sketchfab.com/heynic

car-pack.glb — City Car, Sedan, SUV, Luxury, Supercar, Hypercar and Electric
  platforms, and the street traffic (compact, minivan, off-roader, pickup, wagon)
  Original: "Generic passenger car pack" by Comrade1280
  https://sketchfab.com/comrade1280
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/generic-passenger-car-pack-20f9af9b8a404d5cb022ac6fe87f21f5
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

  Changes: meshopt-compressed, textures resized to 512 px WebP (gltf-transform
  optimize); two textures of the Wagon body, missing from the received file,
  replaced with plain grey. In the game each car is taken out of the pack,
  straightened and scaled, and its paint texture is turned grey so the body
  can be painted in the game's colours.
