Idle Car Empire – Delete your account

You can delete your Idle Car Empire account (including a guest account) and all the data stored with it at any time.

In the game (immediate)

  1. Open Idle Car Empire (the Android app or the web game) on a device where you are signed in.
  2. Tap your avatar (top right) to open your Profile.
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete account, then Delete for good.

By email (if you can't open the game)

Write to CONTACT_EMAIL from the email address of your account, with the subject “Idle Car Empire account deletion” and your player name. The account is deleted within 30 days, and you get a confirmation.

What is deleted

  • Your account (account id, email address, password).
  • Your cloud save (all game progress).
  • Your profile (player name, avatar), your public empire card on the leaderboards and your ghost race car.
  • Gifts you sent and received, your alliance seat and its reward claims (an alliance you led passes to its longest-standing member, or closes if you were the last).

Nothing of it is kept. The anonymous beginner statistics were never linked to your account, so there is nothing of yours in them. The game copy on your device is removed from the game when you delete in the game; otherwise uninstall the app or clear the site's data.

See also the privacy policy.

Ștergerea contului (Română)

În joc: deschide jocul, apasă pe avatar (dreapta sus) → Profil → jos, Șterge contul → Șterge definitiv. Se șterg imediat contul, salvarea din cloud, profilul, fișa publică din clasamente, cadourile și locul în alianță.

Prin email: scrie la CONTACT_EMAIL de pe adresa contului, cu numele de jucător. Contul se șterge în cel mult 30 de zile.
